## Problem: Best Time to Buy and Sell Stock (Easy)
**Link:** https://leetcode.com/problems/best-time-to-buy-and-sell-stock/

### Approach
Maintained a running dynamic minimum buy price while calculating max profit in a single pass through the list.

### Complexity
- Time: O(n)
- Space: O(1)

### Notes
Single pass solution avoids nested loops.
